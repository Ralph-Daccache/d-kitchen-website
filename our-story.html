Three Generations of Excellence.

Led by the third generation of owners, d. Kitchen stands as an uncompromising authority in bespoke kitchen environments. We operate at the intersection of architectural precision and deep material knowledge. Our work is a direct reflection of formal minimalism—where every element is deliberate, structured, and executed with exacting standards.

First Generation: Foundation (1970s)

Establishing the foundation of uncompromising quality and structural understanding in bespoke cabinetry. Every joint, every measurement was an exercise in permanence. The origins of our obsession with architectural precision began here.

ARCHIVAL MATERIAL

Second Generation: Expansion (1990s)

Expanding technical capabilities and forging global partnerships with premium material suppliers. We introduced new stones, refined metals, and engineering techniques that allowed our structures to span wider and stand more boldly.

Material Integrity

The quiet luxury of our environments is achieved through a profound understanding of natural materials. The tactile quality of solid oak and the intrinsic patterns of Calacata marble are framed within rigorous geometric layouts.

Third Generation: Modernism (Present)

Modernizing the aesthetic toward formal minimalism. Integrating advanced architectural integration while honoring the foundational principles of excellence. Today, a d. Kitchen space is an exercise in subtraction—removing the non-essential to reveal pure functional form.

The Future is Formal.

Continued dedication to executing at the highest standard of minimal design.
